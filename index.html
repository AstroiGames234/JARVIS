VOICE ACCESS

About wake phrase

When the app window is visible, it requests its own microphone stream for “Jarvis” listening. When you hide the app, it releases the mic and the tray takes over. Say “Jarvis” followed by a request, or say “Jarvis” and wait for “Yes, sir?” before giving your request.

Questions and camera images are processed by Qwen locally through Ollama. Say “open” followed by an app’s Start menu name to launch it, whether you are using the window or tray. For an installed Steam game, say “launch [game name] through Steam.” Web searches open in your browser. The app and tray coordinate so only one listens at a time.

Allow microphone access for http://127.0.0.1:48123 in Edge and for desktop apps in Windows Privacy settings. Install an English speech recognition language for tray listening. Use the J.A.R.V.I.S. tray menu to show the app or exit and stop listening.
