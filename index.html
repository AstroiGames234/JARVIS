Local setup and privacy
Install Ollama and Node.js, then run Setup-Local-AI.cmd once to download the Qwen model, offline Whisper speech recognition, Kokoro voices, and ready-made wake reply. The speech model adds about 160 MB. Questions, microphone audio, and camera images are processed on this PC. Allow desktop apps to use your microphone in Windows Privacy settings. The app uses its local Whisper recognizer while visible; hiding it hands listening to the tray.
VOICE ACCESS

About wake phrase

When the app window is visible, J.A.R.V.I.S. uses offline Whisper speech recognition to hear “Jarvis” and your request. When you hide the app, it releases the mic and the tray takes over. Say “Jarvis” followed by a request, or say “Jarvis” and wait for “Yes, sir?” before giving your request.

Questions and camera images are processed by Qwen locally through Ollama. Say “open” followed by an app’s Start menu name to launch it, whether you are using the window or tray. For an installed Steam game, say “launch [game name] through Steam.” Web searches open in your browser. The app and tray coordinate so only one listens at a time.

Allow desktop apps to use your microphone in Windows Privacy settings. Use the J.A.R.V.I.S. tray menu to show the app or exit and stop listening.
